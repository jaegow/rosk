# Panel

A card is a panel, in a page, a form or a modal.

- A control acting on the panel's content sits in the panel's footer: its way into the record it shows in the primary lane as `secondary`, actions on its content in the tertiary lane.
- A lone CTA sits in the primary lane, at the right end, as `secondary`.
- The head's CTA part only adds to what the panel lists; an add for a table in a card or section sits in that head, above the table.
- A reference to another record inside a value is plain text, never a link.
- A popover footer fits one line; a further choice is a row in its content.
- A region a parent already insets (an add-row group) uses a chrome-less CTA row.
- What acts on the whole record belongs in the page footer.
