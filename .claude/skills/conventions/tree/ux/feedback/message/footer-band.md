# Footer band

- A short sentence that changes which footer CTA the reader presses, or what they expect it to do, sits in the footer band: plain text, one line, cut with its whole text on hover.
- A read-only footer's "View only" says who can change it in the same line.
- A sentence among the CTAs reads as a control: the CTA row holds controls only.
