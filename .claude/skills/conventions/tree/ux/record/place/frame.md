# Record frame

- A form of four editable fields or fewer and no cards takes one column, in a dialog too; any other form takes two.
- Span a field that needs the width; a lone card with no partner takes the whole row.
- A side-labelled checkbox or switch beside a top-labelled field sits on its input line.
- A group just added from a section's head shows first until the record is saved and read back.
- Give each table in a form its own section, at the end of the form, and the form the width its widest table needs.
- Create and edit are one view in one component.
- Re-seed the form when moving to another record; after a save stay and reset from the response; after a create go to the new record.
