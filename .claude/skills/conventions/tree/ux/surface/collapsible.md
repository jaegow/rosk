# Collapsible containers

A collapsible container is a card, section or step whose head opens and closes its body.

- Lay out its head as icon, label, chips, free space, then the expand icon pinned to the trailing edge, so a stack's icons share one line.
- Show nesting by a left inset only.
- Use one expand glyph app-wide, pointing down closed and up open.
- Open on a press, never on hover.
