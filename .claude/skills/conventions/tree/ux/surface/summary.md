# Summary cards

- A summary card states one figure about the data its page shows; a page's summary cards sit in one band under the heading, above the filters.
- A count is the card's count, never a line of context.
- Link a card that stands for another page; mute a zero figure, never hide it.
- While refreshing, keep the band and mark it busy.
