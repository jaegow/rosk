# Landing

- The root and a group route redirect a reader to the first nav row or tab they hold.
- A signed-in reader no shell admits gets one full-screen page outside every shell: a heading, what they belong to, and Sign Out.
- Show the loading gate while permissions resolve, and the denial to a reader holding no row.
