# Head tabs

A view's tabs switch what a page, a modal, a card or a list column shows.

- Put the tab bar in the view's head on its own full-width row, below the title row and subtitle.
- Tabs switching one part further down a page sit in that part's own card head.
- Below the tabs sits the head's status band, then the content, whose top edge carries the loading bar.
